Questions, answered

How accurate is the AI band score? +

The AI grades against the official public IELTS band descriptors, criterion by criterion. Treat it as a well-calibrated estimate and a diagnosis of what to fix, not a guarantee of your official result — no practice tool can promise that.

Is this for Academic or General Training? +

Both. Reading passages are tagged Academic or General, and the writing evaluator handles Task 1 and Task 2 for either exam type.

Do I need to pay to start? +

No. The free plan includes every reading passage, the full vocabulary builder, and three AI essay evaluations each month. No card required.

Does it work on my phone? +

Yes — BandUp is built mobile-first. Flashcards on the bus, essays at your desk.
